# Local additions to blast-radius

These rules come from this repo's workflow doc. They add to the upstream
instructions in SKILL.md, and they win where the two differ.

- **In a Refactor,** the result feeds `to-tickets`: the risks become the
  behaviour-preserving steps and the checks between them.
- **Several models.** For a big or wide change, ask other model families
  through `second-opinion` and merge their answers.
