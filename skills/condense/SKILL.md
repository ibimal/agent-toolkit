# Condense

Rewrite your previous response to be shorter without making it simpler.

1. Keep every fact, number, condition, caveat, and technical term.
2. Remove repetition, filler, and restated conclusions.
3. Merge sentences that share a subject. Drop examples that repeat a point
   already made.
4. Do not replace exact terms with plainer ones.

Output only the rewritten response.
