# Local additions to polish

These rules come from this repo's workflow doc. They add to the upstream
instructions in SKILL.md, and they win where the two differ.

- **Mode and register.** Always use edit mode and "the user's own writing"
  register from the instructions below.
- **Reader, channel and ask.** Work out who reads it, where (Slack, email, a
  PR comment, a post) and what the user wants from them, or take them from
  the request, as in `/polish for <name> on Slack: ...`.
- **Keep everything; add nothing.** Keep every fact, number, name, ask and
  commitment. Add no claim, promise, apology or warmth the user did not
  write. A fact that would help goes below the rewrite as a suggestion,
  never into it.
- **Point first.** Put the point or the ask first, and turn a vague ask
  ("let me know your thoughts") into one the reader can answer.
- **Length and voice.** Cut filler and hedges, one idea per sentence, at the
  length the channel expects. Run `unslop` for AI tells without polishing the
  user's voice away: professional, not stiff. When the always-on style rules
  are loaded, follow them.
- **Output.** Return the rewrite ready to paste. Give a second version only
  when the tone is a real choice, such as firmer or softer. Never send
  anything.
