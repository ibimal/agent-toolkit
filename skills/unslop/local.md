# Local additions to unslop

These rules come from this repo's workflow doc. They add to the upstream
instructions in SKILL.md, and they win where the two differ.

- **Keep every fact.** Numbers, names, conditions, caveats and commitments
  survive unchanged; only the wording changes.
- **The user's style rules win** where they are loaded and disagree with a
  rule below.
- **Code, identifiers, commands, paths and quoted text** stay exactly as they
  are.
